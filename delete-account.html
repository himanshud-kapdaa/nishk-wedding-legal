Delete your account

For the “Nish & K” wedding guest app

In the app (instant)

  1. Open the Nish & K app and sign in.
  2. On Home, scroll to the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

By email

If you can’t open the app, email himanshu.d@kapdaa.com from the email address you registered with, with the subject “Delete my account”. We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

Everything linked to your account, permanently: your name, email and password, party details, travel details, dining choices and allergens, activity and speech sign-ups, song dedications, guestbook messages, every photo and video you uploaded and your likes. Nothing is kept afterwards.

All remaining guest data is deleted by 31 January 2027 anyway. See the full privacy policy.
