PHUKET · OCT 25–27, 2026

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

“Nish & K” is a private app for the guests of Nish & K’s wedding weekend in Phuket, Thailand (25–27 October 2026). It helps guests share travel plans, choose meals, sign up for activities, receive updates and share photos. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and how you can delete it.

Who is responsible

The app is run by the wedding’s organisers. For any privacy question or request, email himanshu.d@kapdaa.com.

What we collect

Only what you enter in the app:

  • Account: your full name, email address and password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Your party: number of adults and children, and the names of the guests with you.
  • Travel: arrival and departure details — flight number, time, hotel, and whether you need accessibility assistance.
  • Dining: Jain / non-Jain preference, food allergens and your menu choices.
  • Events: activity sign-ups, speech sign-up, song dedications and guestbook messages.
  • Photos and videos you choose to upload, and the photos you “like”.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, a push-notification token for your device.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, advertising ID or browsing activity, has no ads, and uses no analytics or tracking tools.

How we use it

  • To plan the wedding weekend: airport transfers, catering, activity numbers and the speech line-up. Relevant details are shared with the wedding planner, caterer and transport company for that purpose only.
  • To show you your own answers, the event schedule and messages from the organisers.
  • To send wedding updates as notifications, if you allow them.
  • To share the photos and videos you upload with the other signed-in wedding guests. Your name is shown next to your uploads. Other guests never see your email or your other answers.

We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage, hosted in Singapore). Notifications are delivered through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access is limited: each guest can only read their own answers, and only the organisers can see all guests’ answers.

How long we keep it

We keep your data only as long as needed for the wedding. After the event, the couple receives a copy of the shared photos and videos, and all guest data is deleted from the app’s servers by 31 January 2027. You can delete it sooner at any time (below).

Deleting your account and data

In the app, open Home, scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, all your answers, and every photo and video you uploaded — straight away. If you can’t use the app, see how to request deletion by email.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing himanshu.d@kapdaa.com. Answers you can’t change in the app (such as menu choices) can be updated by the planner on request.

Children

The app is meant for adult guests. Children’s names are only entered by the adult they are travelling with, as part of the party details.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
